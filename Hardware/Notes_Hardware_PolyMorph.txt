=> Main FC Board (Positioning sensors, Communications with Companion board and Motor controllers
	Capacitors connected to the ICM42688:
		Decoupling capacitor, purpose = ensure a stable power supply for the sensor. It helps handle potential current bursts (current burst = discharge of the energy stored in the capacitor).
			- 100 nF capacitor connected to VDD (C1) → Noise management related to the sampling frequency (because of the small value).
			- 2.2 µF capacitor connected to VDD (C2) → Low-frequency noise management (because of the larger value).
			- 10 nF capacitor connected to VDDIO (C3) → VDDIO powers the measurement buffer (I/O).
